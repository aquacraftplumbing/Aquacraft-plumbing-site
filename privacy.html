Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

AQUACRAFT PLUMBING INC. respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through aquacraftplumbing.com, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you submit our service-request form, call, text, or email us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, requested service, message, property or service location, and any other information you choose to share.

Our hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate and protect the website, such as an IP address, browser or device type, requested pages, timestamps, and security or spam-detection data.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, schedule or provide plumbing services, communicate about a request, maintain business records, secure the website, prevent spam or misuse, and comply with applicable law.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website and respond to requests, including Netlify, which hosts the website and processes form submissions. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights or safety, or as part of a business transfer.

The website loads fonts provided by Google. Links to Google Reviews and Facebook take you to those companies’ websites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Cookies and tracking

We do not currently use advertising pixels or website analytics cookies. Third-party services needed to deliver website content may use technical storage or receive connection information under their own policies. This website does not currently respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals, and we do not knowingly permit third parties to collect personal information here for cross-site behavioral advertising.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete information you submitted through this website by contacting us. We may need to retain certain records when required for legitimate business or legal purposes. Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have additional privacy rights.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for people seeking plumbing services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will show when the policy was last revised.

Contact us
