Drain cleaning and clog removal

Clear help for slow or blocked drains in homes and light commercial properties across Sonoma, Napa, and Marin counties.

Get water moving again

We clear clogs and slow drains in sinks, tubs, showers, and main lines. We work cleanly, explain what we find, and check that the blockage is resolved before the job is complete.

Recurring backups, several slow fixtures, or unusual drain sounds can point to a larger line problem. We’ll help identify the practical next step.

  • Tubs, showers, and floor drains
  • Main-line clog evaluation
  • Residential and light commercial service
